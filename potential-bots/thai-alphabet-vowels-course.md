# Potential Future Bot — Thai Alphabet & Vowels Course

**Status:** Idea only — not a locked decision, not scheduled, not part of Thai Talk Breaks' current scope.
**Origin:** Surfaced during Thai Talk Breaks' Weeks 2–4 tone-marking cleanup, 20 August 2026, when correcting the นี้/ร้อน mai-tho tone pattern and encountering more advanced rules (e.g. leading-ห consonant clusters like ใหญ่, where ห combines with a low-class consonant to behave as high class for tone purposes) that go meaningfully beyond what a practical spoken-Thai course needs to teach.

## The core idea
A separate course/bot, distinct from Thai Talk Breaks, that actually teaches the Thai writing system — the alphabet, consonant classes, vowel forms, and the tone rules that follow from them. This is the deep-literacy counterpart to Thai Talk Breaks' deliberately practical, non-literacy approach.

## Why this is genuinely a different product, not a Thai Talk Breaks feature
Thai Talk Breaks is explicitly locked to treat Thai script as decorative/reference only, never the tested skill (LDTKB-006, LDTKB-007, and CLAUDE_AI_HANDOFF.md's founding principle that this product is for people who want to speak, not read, Thai). A reading/writing-focused course would invert that positioning entirely — it's a different learner intent, likely a different (though overlapping) audience, and arguably a different product name/brand rather than a feature bolted onto Thai Talk Breaks.

## What it might eventually cover (rough, unrefined)
- The Thai consonant classes (mid/high/low) and why they matter
- Vowel forms and their placement (before, after, above, below the consonant)
- Tone rules, including the more advanced cases skipped in Thai Talk Breaks' practical Karaoke system (e.g. leading-consonant clusters)
- Reading real Thai text, not just romanized Karaoke

## Explicitly not decided
- Whether this becomes its own bot, a Thai Talk Breaks "graduate track," or something else entirely
- Timing — this is a someday idea, not a near-term roadmap item
- Name, pricing, audience, or any other product detail
