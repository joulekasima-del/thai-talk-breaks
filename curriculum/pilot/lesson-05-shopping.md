# Lesson 5 of 7 — Shopping & Paying

**Situation:** Shopping & the market (arc position 5 of 7)
**Phrase taught:** "Can I pay by card?"
**Status:** Reviewed and approved by Joule (native Thai speaker), 19 August 2026 — pending image/audio production

---

## Why this lesson is here (arc rationale)
Natural continuation of the transactional thread from lessons 3–4. Reuses "tâo-rài" (how much, lesson 4) conceptually as part of the same shopping moment, and introduces a genuinely useful, low-pressure practical phrase.

**Revision note:** this lesson originally taught a bargaining phrase ("can you lower the price?"). Replaced per Joule's direction (19 August 2026) — bargaining assumes a posture that doesn't fit every learner, and "can I pay by card?" is a more universally useful, lower-friction shopping phrase for a beginner.

## Delivery sequence (per LDTKB-006)

### Step 1 — Picture
A hand holding a bank card near a small shop or market stall counter.

### Step 2 — Thai Karaoke (gender-branched per LDTKB-024)

| Speaker voice | Karaoke | Tone pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Male | **jàai pàan bàt dâai măi kráp** | low – falling – low – falling – rising – high |
| Female | **jàai pàan bàt dâai măi ká** | low – falling – low – falling – rising – high |

*("jàai" = pay, "pàan" = via/through, "bàt" = card, "dâai măi" = can [you]/is it possible — corrected per Joule, 19 August 2026, to include "ผ่าน" for natural phrasing. Female particle is คะ (ká, high tone), not ค่ะ — this is a question, per Joule's grammar correction, 19 August 2026.)*

### Step 3 — English meaning
"Can I pay by card?"

### Step 4 — Thai script (decorative)
จ่ายผ่านบัตรได้ไหมครับ / จ่ายผ่านบัตรได้ไหมคะ

### Step 5 — Native audio
Two recordings (male/female branch).

### Step 6 — Activity (recognition tap)
Learner hears today's phrase plus 2 distractors from earlier lessons (e.g. lesson 4's "tâo-rài" and lesson 3's "ao an-níi"), taps the one meaning "can I pay by card."
