# Lesson 2 of 7 — Numbers 1–10

**Situation:** Numbers (arc position 2 of 7 — see BUILD_TRACKER.md Stage 3)
**Phrases taught:** Numbers 1–10
**Status:** Pilot-ready — content, native-speaker review, audio, and images all complete (Joule, 19 August 2026)

---

## Why this lesson is here (arc rationale)
Numbers underpin lessons 3–5 (ordering, transport, shopping — quantities, prices, distances). Teaching them now means later lessons can safely reuse them without testing untaught vocabulary (per CLAUDE_AI_HANDOFF.md Section 4's "teach before testing" rule).

## Delivery sequence (per LDTKB-006)

### Step 1 — Picture
**Updated 20 August 2026 to reflect final production** — not the original "ten fingers" concept. Each number is illustrated as a large, decoratively-crafted numeral (material/style varies per number, e.g. gilded woodwork, silk textile) with a playful baby elephant interacting with it, each image also depicting one of 10 major Thai festivals through the year (starting from Songkran). See `curriculum/pilot/pilot-image-prompt-list.md` for the full design rationale and per-number festival mapping.

### Step 2 — Thai Karaoke (per LDTKB-025 tone system)

| # | Karaoke | Tone pattern |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | nùeng | low |
| 2 | sǎwng | rising |
| 3 | sǎam | rising |

| 10 | sìp | low |

*Note: numbers are not gender-branched (no krap/kha involved) — LDTKB-024 branching applies to polite phrase endings, not counting words.*

### Step 3 — English meaning
"One, two, three... ten" (numerals shown alongside each Karaoke entry above)

### Step 4 — Thai script (decorative)
๑ ๒ ๓ ๔ ๕ ๖ ๗ ๘ ๙ ๑๐ (Thai numerals) — or standard Arabic numerals 1–10 alongside Thai script number-words (หนึ่ง สอง สาม...). **Resolved (20 August 2026):** the final images deliberately contain no Thai script at all (an explicit Firefly prompt instruction, after an earlier generation attempt produced garbled/incorrect Thai text on a numeral's base) — the numeral itself is rendered as a plain Arabic digit, and Thai script for each number word lives only in this lesson's own Karaoke/script table, not baked into the artwork.

### Step 5 — Native audio
Ten short clips, one per number. No gender branching needed for this lesson.

### Step 6 — Activity (recognition tap, per LDTKB-026)
Learner hears one number spoken aloud, taps the matching numeral from 3 options (e.g. hears "hâa," taps "5" from options 3/5/8).
