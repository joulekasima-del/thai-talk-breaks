### Step 2 — Thai Karaoke (gender-branched per LDTKB-024)

| Speaker voice | Karaoke | Tone pattern (per LDTKB-025) |
|---|---|---|
| Male | **sà-wàt-dii kráp** | low – low – mid – high |
| Female | **sà-wàt-dii kâ** | low – low – mid – falling |

**Tone legend** (shown once, early in onboarding — per LDTKB-025):

| Mark | Tone | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| a | Mid | flat, calm |
| à | Low | starts low, stays low |
| â | Falling | starts high, drops |
| á | High | tight, high pitch |
| ǎ | Rising | starts low, rises up |
