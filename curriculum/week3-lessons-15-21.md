| Speaker | Karaoke | Tone pattern | Script |
|---|---|---|---|

## Day 21 — Preferences
**Phrase:** I don't like it (statement — ค่ะ; template, activity inserted after)
**Applies:** LDTKB-040's age-relative pronoun system — was missing this on first draft, added during self-review, 20 August 2026 (direct negation of Day 17's "ชอบ" pattern, should match its treatment)

| Speaker | Karaoke | Tone pattern | Script |
|---|---|---|---|
| Male, younger than listener | phǒm mâi chôrp ... kráp | rising – falling – falling – (activity) – high | ผมไม่ชอบ...ครับ |
| Male, older than listener | phîi mâi chôrp ... kráp | falling – falling – falling – (activity) – high | พี่ไม่ชอบ...ครับ |
| Female, younger than listener | nǔu mâi chôrp ... kâ | rising – falling – falling – (activity) – falling | หนูไม่ชอบ...ค่ะ |
| Female, older than listener | phîi mâi chôrp ... kâ | falling – falling – falling – (activity) – falling | พี่ไม่ชอบ...ค่ะ |

**How to build your own sentence:** the blank space after ไม่ชอบ commonly takes one of two forms:
1. **Verb + Object (most common)** — e.g. ไม่ชอบทำงานออฟฟิศ *(mâi-chôrp-tam-ngaan-órf-fít, "don't like working office job")* ⚠️loanword tone uncertain, ไม่ชอบกินของทอด *(mâi-chôrp-gin-khǎwng-thâwt, "don't like eating fried food")*
2. **Object (+ adverb)** — e.g. ไม่ชอบรถติด *(mâi-chôrp-rót-dtìt, "don't like traffic jams")*

To make a complete, natural sentence: pick your form from the table above (matching your gender and relative age), then insert the verb+object or object phrase in place of "...".
