## Day 15 — Introducing yourself
**Phrase:** My name is... (statement — ค่ะ; template phrase, learner's name inserted after)
**Applies:** LDTKB-040's age-relative pronoun system (locked 20 August 2026) — this is the first lesson to use it

| Speaker | Karaoke | Tone pattern | Script |
|---|---|---|---|
| Male, younger than listener | phǒm chêu ... kráp | rising – falling – (name) – high | ผมชื่อ...ครับ |
| Male, older than listener | phîi chêu ... kráp | falling – falling – (name) – high | พี่ชื่อ...ครับ |

## Day 21 — Preferences
**Phrase:** I don't like it (statement — ค่ะ)

| Voice | Karaoke | Tone pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Male | mâi chôrp kráp | falling – falling – high |
| Female | mâi chôrp kâ | falling – falling – falling |

Script: ไม่ชอบครับ / ไม่ชอบค่ะ

**How to build your own sentence:** the blank space after ไม่ชอบ commonly takes one of two forms:
1. **Verb + Object (most common)** — e.g. ไม่ชอบทำงานออฟฟิศ ("don't like working office job"), ไม่ชอบกินของทอด ("don't like eating fried food")
2. **Object (+ adverb)** — e.g. ไม่ชอบรถติด ("don't like traffic jams")

To make a complete, natural sentence, add the pronoun (per LDTKB-040) plus ครับ/ค่ะ at the ends — e.g. a younger male speaker: ผมไม่ชอบรถติดครับ.
