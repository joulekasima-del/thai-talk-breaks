## Day 25 — Asking permission
**Phrase:** May I...? (question — คะ; template, action inserted)

| Voice | Karaoke | Tone pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Male | khǎw ... dâi mǎi kráp | rising – (action) – falling – rising – high |
| Female | khǎw ... dâi mǎi ká | rising – (action) – falling – rising – high |

Script: ขอ...ได้ไหมครับ / ขอ...ได้ไหมคะ

**How to build your own sentence** — the blank space after ขอ takes one of two forms:

1. **Verb + Object (most common)** — e.g. ขอจอดรถตรงนี้ได้ไหมครับ/คะ *(khǎw-jòrt-rót-dtrong-níi-dâai-mǎi-kráp/ká, "may I park here?")*, ขอไปเข้าห้องน้ำได้ไหมครับ/คะ *(khǎw-bpai-khâo-hôrng-náam-dâai-mǎi-kráp/ká, "may I go to the bathroom?")* — corrected from ค่ะ to คะ, this is a question (ได้ไหม), so the female ending must be คะ per the grammar rule, ขอยืมดินสอ 1 แท่งได้ไหมครับ/คะ *(khǎw-yeuum-din-sǎw-nèung-thâeng-dâai-mǎi-kráp/ká, "may I borrow a pencil?")*. You can also replace the ending "ได้ไหมครับ/คะ" with "นะครับ/นะคะ" (per LDTKB-041) — this softens the phrase from asking permission to simply letting the listener know.
2. **Object only** — stronger and more direct, since you're asking to actually receive the item from the person you're speaking to: ขอยาดมอันนี้นะครับ/นะคะ *(khǎw-yaa-dom-an-níi-ná-kráp/ná-ká, "[give me] this inhaler, please")* — male form added, original draft only had the female version, ขอปากกาอันนี้ได้ไหมครับ/คะ *(khǎw-bpaak-gaa-an-níi-dâai-mǎi-kráp/ká, "may I have this pen?")*

## Day 27 — Ways to say thank you
**Phrase:** Thank you very much (statement — ค่ะ)

| Voice | Karaoke | Tone pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Male | khàwp-khun mâak kráp | low – mid – falling – high |
| Female | khàwp-khun mâak kâ | low – mid – falling – falling |

Script: ขอบคุณมากครับ / ขอบคุณมากค่ะ

**Note on มาก (mâak):** this is a very common intensifier placed after an adjective to mean "very" — already seen in Day 11 (ร้อนมาก, *rórn-mâak*, "very hot") and Day 19 (อร่อยมาก, *a-rôi-mâak*, "very delicious"). Worth explicitly calling out here that it's the same reusable word, not a new one, reinforcing the pattern across lessons.

## Day 29 — Review & consolidation

**New vision (per Joule, 20 August 2026):** a semi-comic-book style animated recap — a short story told across the 30-day course, presented as a comic with Thai Karaoke text inside the speaking character's conversation bubbles. Real synced audio narration plays as each page displays, holding on each page for the duration of that page's audio before advancing. One quiz popup appears after the learner finishes watching. The content stays permanently accessible in the bot chat — the learner can revisit it any time, not just once.

**⚠️ This is a significant new feature, not a content-drafting task — flagging honestly rather than pretending otherwise:**
- Nothing built so far (Checkpoints 1–3) handles video, synced audio-to-visual playback, or persistently revisitable rich media in a Telegram chat — this is new technical territory, not an extension of the existing text/image/audio message pattern.
- Telegram does support video messages, but "comic-style pages holding for audio duration, revisitable anytime" implies either a pre-rendered video file (audio + visuals baked together, produced once) or something more interactive — these have very different production paths and effort.
- This needs its own dedicated design and technical-feasibility conversation before it's ready to become a locked decision or a Claude Code checkpoint — it shouldn't be rushed into the same batch as the other 21 straightforward lesson-content days.

**Recommendation:** treat this as a real, exciting future direction — captured here so it's not lost — but hold off on locking exact scope until we've had a focused conversation on what's actually technically feasible to build.
