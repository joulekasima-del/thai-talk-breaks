# Stage 2 — Customer Interview Script

**Version:** 1.0  
**Interview length:** 15–20 minutes  
**Participants required:** Five European and five Russian Telegram-using expatriates  
**Research owner:** Joule

## Research purpose

Learn whether Telegram-using expatriates have a real need for a low-cost, scheduled spoken-Thai product and whether the proposed picture/Karaoke/audio structure is understandable.

The interview is not a sales presentation. Ask about the participant's real behavior before showing the concept.

## Before the interview

- Confirm that the participant is at least 18 years old.
- Confirm that they use Telegram.
- Explain that this is product research, not a Thai lesson or sales call.
- Ask permission to take written notes.
- Ask separately whether an anonymous quotation may be used.
- Do not collect their Telegram username, phone number or legal name in the repository record.
- Assign the next participant ID: `EU-01`–`EU-05` or `RU-01`–`RU-05`.

## Opening statement

> Thank you for helping me. I am exploring a small Telegram product that may help foreigners practise useful spoken Thai. I have not finished the product, and honest criticism is more useful than politeness. I will take notes using an anonymous participant number. You may skip any question or stop at any time.

## Part A — The participant's real experience

Ask these before describing the product.

1. How long have you lived in Thailand, or how often do you visit?
2. How would you describe your current Thai speaking ability?
3. Tell me about the last situation where not speaking Thai caused difficulty, discomfort or dependence on another person.
4. In which three daily situations would speaking a little Thai help you most?
5. What have you already tried to learn Thai?
6. What made you continue or stop using those methods?
7. Do you want to learn to read Thai, speak Thai, or both? Why?
8. How frequently do you normally open Telegram?
9. Which Telegram notifications do you keep turned on, and which do you mute?

### Interviewer rule

Ask “Can you tell me more?” when an answer is important. Do not suggest food, transport, dating, work or shopping before the participant answers independently.

## Part B — Learning and notification behavior

10. If a very short Thai lesson arrived automatically, what time would be least disruptive?
11. Would you prefer one, two or three short notifications each day? Why?
12. How many minutes could you realistically give to one lesson?
13. What would make you mute or block a learning bot?
14. What would make you keep using it for 30 days?
15. Would a notification-sound test during setup feel useful or annoying?

## Part C — Show the product concept

Read or show this neutral description:

> The product would send short spoken-Thai lessons inside Telegram at times selected by the learner. A lesson would show a picture, Thai pronunciation written with English letters, the English meaning, a small Thai-script reference, native Thai audio, and one small activity. It would focus on speaking rather than teaching Thai reading.

Then ask:

16. What is your immediate reaction?
17. What part is most useful?
18. What part is unclear or unnecessary?
19. Please explain the lesson structure back to me in your own words.
20. Would Thai pronunciation written with English letters be enough for you to try speaking?
21. Thai uses tones. Would simple tone cues help, or would they make the Karaoke spelling look too difficult?
22. Would the small Thai-script reference add authenticity, create confusion, or make no difference?
23. What would you need to trust the pronunciation?
24. Would you prefer instructions in English, Russian, or another language?

## Part D — Purchase evidence

Do not ask only “Would you buy it?” Hypothetical agreement is weak evidence.

25. If the product were available today, what would you compare it with before deciding?
26. What might stop you from paying for it?
27. Imagine a free seven-day version worked well for you. How would you feel about one small payment of approximately US$6.50 for the complete 30-day course?
28. Would that price feel suspiciously cheap, reasonable, or still not worth paying? Why?
29. Would you be more likely to buy through Telegram Stars, a familiar payment method outside Telegram, or not at all?
30. If invited to a free seven-day pilot, what would prevent you from completing it?
31. May I contact you privately later about the free pilot? Record the contact permission outside the repository.

## Closing

> Thank you. Is there anything about learning practical Thai in Thailand that I should have asked but did not?

After the conversation, complete the record immediately while the details are fresh. Separate exact participant statements from Joule's interpretations.

## Interview quality checklist

- [ ] The participant spoke more than the interviewer.
- [ ] Real past behavior was discussed before the concept.
- [ ] No purchase or positive answer was pressured.
- [ ] Important exact quotations are labelled clearly.
- [ ] Interpretations are separated from observations.
- [ ] No identifying information is placed in the repository.
- [ ] Contact and quotation permissions are recorded correctly.
