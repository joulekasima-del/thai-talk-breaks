# Thai Talk Breaks — Locked Decisions

**Register version:** 1.6  
**Last updated:** 18 August 2026  
**Authority:** Joule

This file contains confirmed decisions only. It must not convert suggestions or research findings into approved product requirements.

## LDTKB-001 — Product category

**Status:** Locked  
**Decision:** The product is a low-cost, notification-led spoken-Thai learning product delivered through Telegram. It is not designed as a Thai reading course.  
**Locked by:** Joule’s confirmation on 18 August 2026.

## LDTKB-002 — Initial user group

**Status:** Locked  
**Decision:** The initial user group is expatriates who already use Telegram. European and Russian Telegram communities are the first customer-research groups.  
**Boundary:** This does not yet prove that either group will buy; the assumption must be validated through interviews.  
**Locked by:** Joule’s confirmation on 18 August 2026.

## LDTKB-003 — Accessibility and convenience principle

**Status:** Locked  
**Decision:** The product should be easy to access inside Telegram and simple enough that the learner does not need to remember to open a separate learning application.  
**Locked by:** Joule’s confirmation on 18 August 2026.

## LDTKB-004 — Learner-configured schedule

**Status:** Locked  
**Decision:** Learners choose the times when their lessons are sent. The bot sends each lesson at the configured time.  
**Operational limitation:** The bot cannot guarantee that a phone produces sound because that is controlled by Telegram, phone notification settings, mute state, Focus/Do Not Disturb and device volume.  
**Locked by:** Joule’s confirmation on 18 August 2026.

## LDTKB-005 — Notification test

**Status:** Locked  
**Decision:** Onboarding includes a notification test so the learner can confirm that the Telegram message arrives and its notification sound is audible before scheduled learning begins.  
**Locked by:** Joule’s confirmation of the eight-stage plan on 18 August 2026.

## LDTKB-006 — Lesson presentation structure

**Status:** Locked  
**Decision:** The core learner-facing sequence is:

1. picture;
2. Thai Karaoke pronunciation;
3. English meaning;
4. Thai written form shown as a decorative/reference element;
5. native Thai audio;
6. a small completion, speaking, recognition or recall activity.

**Boundary:** Learners are not required to learn Thai reading in this product.  
**Locked by:** Joule’s confirmation on 18 August 2026.

## LDTKB-007 — Thai Karaoke is the teaching surface

**Status:** Locked  
**Decision:** Thai Karaoke/romanized pronunciation is the primary written learning surface. Thai script supports cultural identity and reference but is not the tested skill.  
**Open implementation question:** The exact transliteration and tone-cue standard remains to be researched and tested with learners.  
**Locked by:** Joule’s confirmation on 18 August 2026.

## LDTKB-008 — Low-friction price intention

**Status:** Locked  
**Decision:** The product is intentionally inexpensive, targeting a price that feels close to US$6.50 and does not require a high-consideration purchase decision. The initial Telegram test price is 500 Stars.  
**Boundary:** Five hundred Stars correspond to approximately US$6.50 of stated developer reward, not necessarily the buyer’s exact checkout cost. The customer-visible price must be tested before advertising a dollar amount.  
**Locked by:** Joule’s confirmation on 18 August 2026.

## LDTKB-009 — Value is not communicated through a high price

**Status:** Locked  
**Decision:** The commercial strategy does not use a high price to signal quality. Value is communicated through relevance, convenience, reliable scheduled delivery, understandable pronunciation, useful daily situations and a frictionless experience.  
**Locked by:** Joule’s confirmation on 18 August 2026.

## LDTKB-010 — Founder and production model

**Status:** Locked  
**Decision:** Joule is the primary product creator and operator. AI assistants and AI coding agents support research, curriculum structuring, design, implementation, testing and documentation. Joule retains product authority and approves all locked decisions.  
**Locked by:** Joule’s statement on 18 August 2026.

## LDTKB-011 — Business formation approach

**Status:** Locked  
**Decision:** The project proceeds without forming a limited company during validation. A free pilot may be developed first. Before regular public paid sales, Joule will verify and complete the appropriate individual commercial registration and establish tax records.  
**Boundary:** Operating without a company does not remove personal income-tax, consumer, privacy, platform or other applicable obligations.  
**Locked by:** Joule’s confirmation of the eight-stage plan on 18 August 2026.

## LDTKB-022 — Customer interviews deferred; lesson production unblocked

**Status:** Locked  
**Decision:** Stage 2 customer interviews (LDTKB-012) are deferred rather than required as a gate before lesson production. Joule proceeds directly to Stage 3 (creating the seven pilot lessons), drawing on the locked multi-modal lesson structure (LDTKB-006) and Joule's own direct product judgment in place of formal interview evidence.  
**Boundary:** This decision removes the interview gate as a *prerequisite* to lesson production. It does not retroactively validate pricing, notification tolerance, or Karaoke/tone-cue comprehension with the target EU/RU audience — those questions remain genuinely open and untested. Joule may reintroduce interviews, informal user feedback, or another validation method at any later stage at his discretion.  
**Supersedes:** LDTKB-012 (interview-before-lessons requirement) and amends the stage order in LDTKB-017 accordingly — Stage 2 is no longer a hard gate for Stage 3.  
**Locked by:** Joule's explicit confirmation on 18 August 2026.
